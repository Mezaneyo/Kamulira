Why this work matters to me

Many students lose marks, not because they don't know the content, but because they don't know how the exam asks for it. Many teachers were never shown how to write a good test question.

Having worked with the bodies that set and shape national exams, I have seen both sides. I now help teachers and students close that gap, in plain language and with real examples.

"Knowing the content is half the exam. Knowing how to answer is the other half."
